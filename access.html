Access & security

Intercoms, entry fobs and garage access

Door entry system

Intercom

The door buzzer is set up to call a sequence of phone numbers. When you answer, press “0” to speak to the caller, then “3” to open the door.

The concierge can update your number if it changes.

Fobs

Lost fobs can be replaced by speaking to the concierge.

Garage doors

The garage doors for the car park operate on a separate, phone-based system. Call the number to open the door.

If you have a car park space, the concierge can add your phone number to the system.
