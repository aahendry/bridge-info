Access & Security

Intercoms, entry fobs and garage access

Door entry system

Intercom

The door buzzer systems are set up to be put through to a phone number. It phones each number in order. On answering, you can press “0” to speak to the caller, and then “3” to open the door.

The concierge can arrange for updates to be made if you change your number.

Fobs

Lost fobs can be replaced by speaking to the concierge.

Garage doors

The garage doors for the car park operate on a separate, phone-based system. Call the number to open the door.

If you have a car park space, the concierge can add your phone number to the system.
