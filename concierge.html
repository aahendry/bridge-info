Concierge

Where to start with building access, deliveries, and shared-area issues.

What the concierge can help with

Contact the concierge for help with building access, deliveries, or reporting an issue in a shared area. They can help with any issues relating to the day to day running of the building.

Desk location and hours

The concierge desk is located in the main lobby and is usually staffed Monday - Friday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

A night watch is usually available on Friday and Saturday evenings into the following morning.
