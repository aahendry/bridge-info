Bins & recycling

Sort household waste correctly and keep the shared bin area clear.

General waste

There are bin chutes in the upper courtyard. If the chute is blocked, please contact the concierge to report it.

If your rubbish does not fit in the chute, go down to the bin store and put it in directly. No waste is to be left on the floor next to the chutes.

Recycling

Large, blue recycling bins can be found in the corridor on the West side of the upper car park level. Boxes and packaging should be flattened to save space.

Please see the council's guidance on what you can and cannot recycle: https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/13729/What-goes-in-your-bin

There is no glass recycling in the development. The nearest glass recycling bin can be found outside 400 Argyle Street (out the main door and turn right, across the road).

Bulk waste

Bulk waste collection (furniture, appliances, etc.) can sometimes be arranged by the concierge, who should be able to advise.

Private companies can also handle bulk waste collection.
