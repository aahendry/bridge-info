Water & leaks

What to check if a leak starts and how to isolate your flat's supply.

Find your stopcock

The water stopcock is located in the common landing, in a small hatch above the front door. This shuts off all water to the flat. The hatch can be opened with a screwdriver, a coin, a key, or similar.

Water ingress

If water is entering your flat from above, attempt to contact the concierge in the first instance.

Flats above you can shut off their water supply to reduce the flow of water from burst pipes or faulty appliances. If you can identify the flat above you, contact the resident directly to ask them to shut off their stopcock.

The factor maintains insurance for the building's structure. Contact them to report any damage and find out what to do next.

Water heater

The flats in the building generally feature an unvented hot water cylinder, although yours may have been replaced.

These require to be serviced regularly to ensure corrosion does not cause leaks.

A faulty boiler can cause hot water to continuously flow down the overflow drain, which can often be seen outside the door to your block. If you see this happening, pelase check you boiler cupboard for water flowing through the relief pipe, which can be seen as a small open part of a vertical pipe.

Balcony

If your flat has a balcony, you need to ensure that drainage is kept free-flowing.

The balcony has a drain hole, located externally under the railings. Ensure it does not get blocked by leaves/moss, as it could cause the balcony to flood and water to leak through to the properties below.
